Air conditioner problems we repair in Barrie

A Barrie AC works a short season and spends the rest of the year buried. That shapes what fails.

  • It hummed on the first hot day, then nothing

    Months of freeze and thaw are hard on a run capacitor, and the first real demand in June is when it lets go. The compressor hums, can't start and trips. We test the capacitor and contactor, replace what failed and check the compressor amperage so you know it survived.

  • The top grille is dented and the fan rattles

    Ice sliding off a steel or shingle roof lands on whatever is below, and an AC under the eaves takes the hit. A bent fan blade wobbles, chews its motor bearing and can strike the shroud. We replace the blade or motor, straighten crushed fins and talk about a better spot or a stand if the unit is in the slide path.

  • The unit sits tilted on a heaved pad

    Frost heave lifts one corner of a ground pad every winter. A tilted condenser strains the line set at the service valves, which is where leaks start, and it hurts oil return to the compressor. We relevel or replace the pad, or move the unit to a raised stand, and pressure test the lines.

  • Chewed wires and a nest in the cabinet

    A warm condenser cabinet is prime winter real estate for mice. They strip the low-voltage wiring and pack the base with nesting that blocks drainage. We clean it out, repair the wiring and seal the obvious entry points.

  • Cool-ish air and frost on the copper line

    A refrigerant leak, often at a joint stressed by ice load or a heaved pad, or a dirty coil starving the system of airflow. We find the leak with nitrogen and a detector, repair it, pull a vacuum and weigh in a fresh charge rather than topping up every summer.

  • Main floor fine, second floor an oven

    Painswick and Holly two-storeys with one system and a basement thermostat are the classic case. The problem is airflow and balance, not always the AC. We check the blower speed, dampers and coil before anyone talks about a bigger unit.

  • Cold but clammy near the lake

    Humid air off Kempenfelt Bay needs an AC that runs long enough to wring it out. An oversized unit cools fast, shuts off and leaves the moisture. We check cycle times and refrigerant charge, and if the unit really is oversized, we say so when it is time to replace.

  • Alectra bill jumped, cooling didn't

    Cottonwood fluff, grass clippings and a winter's worth of grit on the condenser coil make the unit run longer for less. A proper coil cleaning and a charge check usually brings the bill back down.

  • Sound familiar?

    Describe the problem and we'll tell you what it usually is, what it costs to fix, and whether it's worth fixing.

What's included in a Barrie AC repair call

Replacing a dead AC in Barrie: why a heat pump usually wins

Barrie cools for a few months and heats for the rest of the year. When an air conditioner finally dies, spending the money on a machine that only works in summer leaves a lot on the table.

A cold-climate heat pump costs more than a basic AC up front, but it does both jobs. In July it is a central air conditioner with a variable-speed compressor that runs long, quiet cycles and pulls humidity out of lakefront air properly. From October to April it takes over most of the heating from your Enbridge furnace in a hybrid setup, or from baseboards in an all-electric home.

Then there is the rebate. A plain AC gets nothing. A qualifying heat pump can bring back up to $7,500 through the Home Renovation Savings Program for electric, propane or oil heated homes, and commonly up to $2,000 for Enbridge gas homes. Frequently asked questions

How much does AC repair cost in Barrie?

AC repair cost in Barrie depends on the part that failed, so we quote every repair in writing after diagnosis and before any work. Capacitors, contactors and fan motors are modest repairs; a compressor or evaporator coil on an old unit can approach the price of a new system, and at that point we show you the replacement options, including a cold-climate heat pump from $5,500 installed.

How fast can you get to an AC repair in Barrie?

We book Barrie AC repairs seven days a week, with emergency service in the evenings and on holidays when there is no cooling and someone in the home is at risk from heat. We give you a realistic window when you call rather than a promise we can't keep during a heat wave.

Why won't my air conditioner start after a Barrie winter?

An air conditioner that won't start after winter most often has a failed run capacitor, a stuck contactor, chewed wiring from rodents or a tripped breaker. Check the breaker and the outdoor disconnect first, then call us. Do not keep trying to start a humming compressor, because that can damage it.

Should I cover my AC for the winter in Barrie?

A breathable top cover or a piece of plywood weighted over the fan keeps roof-slide ice and heavy snow out of the fan and fins. Never wrap the whole unit in plastic, because trapped moisture rusts the cabinet and mice love a sealed, dry box. Raising the unit onto a stand does more good than any cover.

Is it worth repairing a 15-year-old air conditioner in Barrie?

A capacitor or fan motor on a 15-year-old AC is usually worth fixing. A compressor or major refrigerant leak on a unit that age usually is not, and in Barrie the replacement that makes the most sense is often a cold-climate heat pump, which cools in summer, heats most of the winter and qualifies for a rebate.

Can I finance an AC replacement in Barrie?

Yes. Financing runs 0% for 3, 6 or 9 months on approved credit, with open loans from 2.99% OAC. If you replace the AC with a qualifying cold-climate heat pump, the rebate from the Home Renovation Savings Program comes back after installation, and we handle the paperwork.
