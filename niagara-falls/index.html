Our pre-season cooling check for Niagara Falls rental units

An AC that quits on a full weekend can cost you a booking and a bad review. Before the hot weather arrives, this is what our technicians go over on each unit.

  • A written list of anything worn, with a price to fix it

Humidity, not just heat: the real summer load in Niagara Falls

A Niagara Falls summer is as much about moisture as it is about temperature. Every air conditioner does two jobs at once: it lowers the temperature, and it pulls water out of the air as the air passes over the cold indoor coil. If the system only does the first job well, you end up with a house that's cool but clammy.

The usual cause is oversizing. A unit that's too big cools the rooms in a few minutes and shuts off before the coil has time to wring out much water. A correctly sized, variable-speed AC or heat pump runs longer at a lower speed, which removes far more moisture and keeps temperatures steady.

We size from a heat gain calculation that counts windows, sun exposure and the people using the space. That matters in an older home in Drummondville and in a newer subdivision house with big west-facing glass alike.

  • Heat gain calculation before any AC or heat pump quote
  • Variable-speed equipment that runs long, low cycles
  • Return air and duct leaks checked so humid basement air isn't pulled upstairs
  • A heat pump that also covers spring and fall heating for lower winter bills

Frequently asked questions

What size air conditioner does a Niagara Falls home need?

The right size comes from the home's heat gain, and square footage alone won't tell you that. We calculate it from windows, insulation, sun exposure and occupancy, because an oversized AC leaves the house cold and clammy.

Why does my house feel cold but damp when the AC runs?

The AC is most likely oversized and shutting off before it can remove moisture. A correctly sized variable-speed system runs longer, slower cycles that dry the air as well as cool it.

How can I keep a short-term rental's AC from failing mid-season?

Book a pre-season check in spring, keep the condensate drain clear and change filters between stays. Most mid-summer failures start as small worn parts that a technician can spot early.

Can a heat pump replace my central air in Niagara Falls?

Yes. A heat pump cools exactly like a central air conditioner and also heats through spring, fall and much of the winter, with your furnace as backup if you keep it.

If the AC dies on a long weekend, can I get AC repair in Niagara Falls?

Yes, holiday breakdowns are part of our emergency service, along with evenings and weekends. Phone 1-647-694-5845, and we'll be upfront about how soon someone can be at the door.

What's the entry price for heat pump installation in Niagara Falls?

A cold-climate heat pump begins at $5,500 installed. Installed pricing depends on size and installation requirements, and qualifying homes may get a rebate, so we confirm eligibility before installation.
