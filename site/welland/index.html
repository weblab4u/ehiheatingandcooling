Furnace repair in Welland and the other problems we fix

These are the calls we get most from older homes and newer subdivisions alike. Several of them cost far less to fix than people expect.

  • Someone told you to replace the furnace and you're not convinced

    We'll take a second look. Our TSSA-certified gas technicians inspect the heat exchanger, burners, inducer and controls, show you what they find and tell you straight whether repair is still reasonable.

  • The furnace clicks on, runs a minute and shuts off again

    Short cycling is often a dirty flame sensor, a clogged filter or a blocked vent pipe, all inexpensive fixes. If the furnace is badly oversized for the house, we'll explain that too.

  • The AC still runs, but your July hydro bill keeps climbing

    An air conditioner from the early 2000s uses a lot more power than a current model with a higher SEER2 rating. We show you the running cost difference so you can decide if replacement makes sense now or later.

  • A white crust of ice coats the outdoor pipe on a hot afternoon

    That points to low airflow or low refrigerant. We check the filter and blower first, then test for leaks with licensed refrigeration techs, instead of simply topping it up and leaving.

  • Rooms at the back of your wartime house are always cold

    Small houses from that era often have short, undersized duct runs and one return. Balancing the ducts or adding a return can fix it, and for a stubborn room a single ductless head is an option.

  • You'd replace the old system now if it weren't for the upfront cost

    0% financing for 3, 6 or 9 months spreads the payments, and a heat pump rebate can take a big bite out of the total. We lay out both in your quote so you can see the real number.

  • Sound familiar?

    Describe the problem and we'll tell you what it usually is, what it costs to fix, and whether it's worth fixing.

Frequently asked questions

When does a Welland furnace stop being worth repairing?

Repair it if the furnace is under about 15 years old, the heat exchanger is sound and the fix is a small part. Replace it if it's past 20 years, needs repeated repairs or has a cracked heat exchanger.

How does 0% financing on a new furnace work?

You pay the cost in equal instalments over 3, 6 or 9 months with no interest, on approved credit. Longer open loans start from 2.99% OAC, and rates are subject to lender approval.

Can I get a second opinion on a furnace replacement quote?

Yes. Our HVAC technician in Welland will inspect the furnace, tell you whether replacement is really needed and give you our own written price.

My Welland home has gas heat. What rebate can a heat pump get?

A smaller one than electric or oil homes get: commonly up to $2,000 with Enbridge gas heat, versus up to $7,500 for homes heated by electricity, oil, propane or wood. Whichever tier applies, the paperwork must be approved before installation day, and the program's terms may change.

Will you beat quotes from other HVAC companies in Welland?

Where applicable, we may undercut a comparable quote from a reputable contractor by up to 10%. Send us the quote and we'll compare it equipment for equipment.

What is AFUE?

AFUE, or annual fuel utilization efficiency, is the share of the gas a furnace turns into heat for your home. A 96% AFUE furnace wastes about 4% of its fuel, compared with 20% or more for many older models.
