Old-furnace warning signs our Ingersoll HVAC technician checks

These are the signs that usually lead to a call. Here's what each one means.

  • The furnace makes a small bang when it lights

    Delayed ignition usually means dirty burners or a weak ignitor, and it needs attention soon. Our TSSA-certified gas techs clean and test the burners and safety controls.

  • There's rust on the bottom of the furnace cabinet

    Rust often comes from a leaking condensate line or AC coil sitting above the furnace. We trace the water, fix the drain and check the heat exchanger for damage.

  • The AC and furnace went in the same year and both look tired

    Replacing them together often costs less than two separate jobs. A heat pump can take the AC's place and cut your gas use at the same time.

  • Someone said you'd need all-new ducts for a heat pump

    Not always. Many existing duct systems handle a properly sized heat pump without trouble. We test static pressure to see whether yours can, and fix the weak spots if not.

  • The rebate rules seem confusing

    In plain terms: get pre-approval first, use a qualifying cold-climate heat pump, and the amount depends on how your home is heated now. We fill in every form.

  • A couple of rooms are always colder than the rest

    Blocked or undersized returns, leaky ducts or a blower set too low are the usual causes. We find which one and fix it, often without any new equipment.

  • Sound familiar?

    Describe the problem and we'll tell you what it usually is, what it costs to fix, and whether it's worth fixing.

Frequently asked questions

Can a heat pump use my existing ducts in Ingersoll?

Usually, yes. We test the ducts first, and if airflow is too restricted we fix the weak spots before installing.

Is furnace installation in Ingersoll within reach on a tight budget?

Often, yes. Expect a starting point of $3,300 installed for one of our high-efficiency gas furnaces, and 0% financing over 3, 6 or 9 months OAC can spread it out. Installed pricing depends on size and installation requirements, and the quote is guaranteed.

What rebate can I get for a heat pump in Ingersoll?

Enbridge gas-heated homes commonly qualify for up to $2,000, and homes heated by electricity, oil or propane may get up to $7,500. Pre-approval must be in place before installation, and program terms can change.

My furnace and AC are the same age. Should I replace both in one go?

If both are near the end, usually yes. One install costs less than two, and it's the best moment to add a heat pump.

Are Thamesford, Beachville and Embro part of your service area?

Yes. Our crews travel from our Stouffville office to Ingersoll and the surrounding Oxford County townships.

Is emergency HVAC in Ingersoll available on long weekends and holidays?

Yes. We take emergency calls evenings, weekends and holidays, and you'll see a written price before any repair.
