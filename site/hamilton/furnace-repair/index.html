Furnace problems we fix in Hamilton homes

The symptom you notice, what it usually means in this city's housing, and what the technician does about it. Our Hamilton HVAC page covers heat pumps, ductless and the rest.

  • No heat on the windiest night of the year on the Mountain

    High-efficiency furnaces vent through PVC on an outside wall. Wind-driven snow on a north or west wall can block the intake or exhaust, and the pressure switch shuts the furnace down to protect you. We clear the terminations, test the switch and the inducer, and if it keeps happening we extend or re-route the venting so it stops.

  • The furnace in your damp lower-city basement is rusting out

    Stone foundations and low basements near the bay hold moisture. Rust on the burners and flame sensor causes failed ignition, and a corroded bottom panel can hint at a rusted heat exchanger. We clean the burners, test the sensor and inspect the exchanger with a camera before we call it safe.

  • Your chimney-vented 80% furnace is backdrafting

    Brick chimneys in century homes were built for coal and oil. Without a sound liner, flue gases can spill back into the basement. We test draft and spillage, check your CO alarms, and quote a liner or a direct-vent high-efficiency replacement.

  • A builder furnace in Binbrook or Waterdown keeps flashing a pressure switch code

    Builder-grade furnaces between 10 and 15 years old often have a condensate trap full of sludge or a tired inducer motor. We clear the trap and drain, test the switch under load and replace only what has failed.

  • The furnace runs all day and the house still won't reach 21

    In an uninsulated double-brick home the furnace may be keeping up by the numbers while leaky duct joints and drafts give it all away. We measure temperature rise, check the ducts and give you a straight answer on whether the furnace or the house is the problem.

  • Short cycling started after you insulated the attic

    Good news: your house needs less heat now. Bad news: the old furnace is oversized for it and trips its limit switch every few minutes. A two-stage furnace sized to the new heat loss fixes it, and we show you that price beside the repair.

  • The furnace works, but the add-on AC coil chokes it

    A central air coil set above an old furnace can restrict airflow until the high limit trips in winter. We clean the coil, check filter sizing and measure static pressure so the furnace can breathe again.

    Describe the problem and we'll tell you what it usually is, what it costs to fix, and whether it's worth fixing.

What's included in a Hamilton furnace repair call

Our furnace repair and tune-up page lists the annual maintenance checklist. A repair call in Hamilton covers the fault plus these checks.

  • Full ignition sequence test: ignitor, flame sensor, gas valve and control board
  • Pressure switch and inducer motor tested under load
  • Heat exchanger inspected for cracks and corrosion
  • Carbon monoxide checked at the registers before we leave
  • Condensate trap and drain cleared on high-efficiency furnaces
  • Intake and exhaust venting checked for ice, snow and blockages
  • Filter size and airflow checked against the blower
  • Written report with the fault found, the fix made and anything to watch

Repair or replace: the rule we use for Hamilton furnaces

Furnace age tells half the story in Hamilton. The other half is what the furnace is attached to. A 15-year-old unit venting through a sound chimney liner in Dundas is a different case from a 15-year-old unit in a lower-city basement where the chimney has been crumbling since the 1990s.

Our working rule: if the furnace is under 12 years old and the failed part is an ignitor, flame sensor, capacitor, pressure switch or drain, repair it. If it is past 15 and the part is a heat exchanger, control board, inducer or blower motor, we put a replacement number beside the repair and let you decide. A new 96% AFUE two-stage furnace burns noticeably less Enbridge gas than a 1990s single-stage unit, and you restart the warranty clock.

Where the house has radiators or almost no ductwork, the replacement conversation often turns into a cold-climate heat pump conversation instead. We'll say so if it fits, and we'll say so if it doesn't.

  • Under 12 years with a small part: repair
  • Over 15 years with a major part: compare both prices
  • Cracked heat exchanger at any age: replace
  • Chimney in bad shape: factor in a liner or go direct-vent

Frequently asked questions

How much does furnace repair cost in Hamilton?

Furnace repair in Hamilton is quoted in writing after diagnosis, because the price depends on the part. Ignitors, flame sensors and capacitors sit at the low end; blower motors, inducers and control boards cost more. If replacement makes more sense, a new high-efficiency furnace starts at $3,300 installed.

Can you repair my furnace tonight if it stops during a cold snap?

We take emergency furnace repair calls in Hamilton on evenings, weekends and holidays. Call 1-647-694-5845, tell us the symptom and where you are, and we'll tell you honestly when a technician can get there.

Why does my furnace on the Mountain shut off when it's windy?

A high-efficiency furnace whose intake or exhaust pipe faces the wind can lose pressure or fill with blown snow, and the pressure switch shuts it down. Clearing the terminations restores heat; extending or re-routing the vent keeps it from happening again.

Is it worth fixing a furnace in a 100-year-old Hamilton house?

Usually yes, if the furnace itself is under about 12 years old and the chimney or venting is sound. The age of the house matters less than the age of the furnace and the condition of the flue. We check both on the first visit and quote repair and replacement side by side.

Do you do furnace tune-ups in Hamilton as well as repairs?

Yes. An annual fall tune-up cleans the burners and flame sensor, tests the safeties and inspects the heat exchanger, and most manufacturers require it to keep extended warranty coverage valid. Ask for tune-up pricing when you call.

Can I finance a replacement furnace if the repair isn't worth it?

Yes. If the diagnosis turns into a new furnace, financing runs 0% for 3, 6 or 9 months, or open loans from 2.99% on approved credit. Rates are subject to lender approval.
