A landlord's checklist from an HVAC contractor in Peterborough

Student houses near Trent and Fleming work hard. Before you choose equipment or approve a repair, these are the points we go through with owners.

  • A furnace or heat pump sized for the whole house, including the converted rooms
  • Bedroom doors that can close without starving the room of air, thanks to proper returns
  • A filter size that's easy to buy and simple for tenants to change
  • A smart thermostat with sensible limits and alerts sent to your phone
  • Working carbon monoxide alarms outside sleeping areas, as Ontario requires in homes with fuel-burning appliances
  • Bigger jobs booked into the gap between leases where possible
  • Service records and warranty registration sent straight to the owner

Ductless heating and cooling for cottages around Lakefield and Bridgenorth

Lots of Kawartha cottages were built for July. Then the owners start coming up in May and staying until Thanksgiving, and the baseboards or wood stove can't keep up with frosty mornings. Some families want the place usable in winter too.

Often the easiest fix is a ductless heat pump. One outdoor unit and one to four indoor heads warm the cottage on cold spring and fall days and cool it in summer, with no ductwork to add. Cold-climate models like the Midea EVOX G3 are rated to heat down to -30°C, so a well-insulated cottage can stay in use all winter, with backup heat for the harshest nights.

Cottages raise their own install questions, and we sort them out on the first visit: panel capacity and room for a 240V circuit, a stand tall enough to clear the snow, and a spot sheltered from wind off the water. A Wi-Fi controller lets you warm the place up before you leave town.

  • Each indoor head matched to its room's heat loss
  • Raised stand for snow and spring runoff
  • Wi-Fi control so you can check in from home
  • Rebate eligibility confirmed before we install

In-town upgrades: get a Peterborough HVAC quote for a furnace or heat pump

Century homes in the core and The Avenues, post-war houses and newer subdivisions on the edges of the city all need different things. A drafty older home may do best with a hybrid setup, where a heat pump does most of the work and a high-efficiency furnace handles snowy cold snaps. A newer home with good ducts might suit a ducted cold-climate heat pump.

Peterborough runs colder than the lakeshore, so we size from a heat-loss calculation, not the label on the old furnace. You get one guaranteed price, your rebate estimate and your financing options in writing.

  • Lennox ML296V and Midea MGH96 furnaces, 96% AFUE and up
  • Heat pumps rated to keep heating in -30°C weather
  • We look after the rebate pre-approval
  • Three, six or nine months at 0% interest, OAC

Frequently asked questions

Do you work with landlords who own student rentals in Peterborough?

Yes. We repair and upgrade heating and cooling in rental houses near Trent and Fleming, send quotes and records to the owner, and arrange access with tenants once you approve.

Can a ductless heat pump heat a Kawartha cottage in winter?

Yes, if the cottage is insulated well enough and the system is a cold-climate model sized from a heat-loss calculation. We plan backup heat for the coldest nights, and an uninsulated summer cottage usually needs insulation work first.

Is heat pump installation in Peterborough worth it if I have natural gas?

It can be. A hybrid system lets the heat pump cover most of the year and adds efficient cooling, while the gas furnace takes over in deep cold, and Enbridge gas-heated homes may qualify for a smaller rebate.

Can I control my cottage heat pump from home?

Yes. We can add a Wi-Fi controller or smart thermostat so you can check the temperature, adjust it and get alerts from your phone.

Who do I call if the furnace dies in a Peterborough rental on a weekend?

The number to call is 1-647-694-5845. We take emergency heating calls evenings, weekends and holidays, and we'll be honest about when a technician can get there.

Are Lakefield and Bridgenorth inside your service area?

Yes. Our crews serve Lakefield, Bridgenorth and the rest of the Peterborough area from our office in Stouffville, and we schedule visits with you.
