Kitchener-Waterloo HVAC problems landlords and parents know well

Rental owners and families call about different things, but the fixes come from the same place: find the cause, then fix it once.

  • A tenant texts you at 11 pm because there's no heat

    We take emergency calls evenings, weekends and holidays. With your OK we book the visit with the tenant directly, and you get the diagnosis and a written price before any repair starts.

  • The same furnace part fails every winter

    Repeat flame sensor or pressure switch calls usually point to a cause upstream: a clogged filter, a blocked condensate line or a venting issue. We fix the cause so you stop paying for the same visit.

  • Filters never get changed between tenants

    We can fit a deeper media filter cabinet that lasts several months between changes, and book your annual tune-up when the unit turns over in spring.

  • The basement apartment is always colder than upstairs

    One furnace and one thermostat can't please two households. A ductless head in the lower unit gives that tenant their own heat and cooling without rebalancing the whole house.

  • The thermostat gets set to 28°C with the windows open

    A smart thermostat with setpoint limits keeps the system in a sensible range and lets you see problems from your phone. Qualifying smart thermostats can earn a $100 rebate.

  • Your 2000s family home still has the original builder furnace

    Twenty-year-old builder units often need more repairs each year. We price the repair and the replacement side by side, including two-stage, variable-speed Lennox and Midea furnaces, so the choice is clear.

  • Five tenants, one thermostat and a nightly argument over the setting

    A student house near the universities often has bedrooms on three floors sharing a single control in the hallway. A ductless head in the hottest or coldest rooms gives those tenants their own setting, and a smart thermostat with locked limits keeps the main system in a sensible range. Both cost less than the calls you're fielding now.

  • Sound familiar?

    Describe the problem and we'll tell you what it usually is, what it costs to fix, and whether it's worth fixing.

Keeping HVAC records for a Kitchener-Waterloo rental

Landlords get asked for heating records more often than they expect: by tenants, by insurers after a claim, and by buyers when the house sells. We make the file easy to keep.

  • Every repair comes with a written diagnosis, the parts replaced and the date, sent to you by email
  • New equipment is registered in your name, with the serial numbers and warranty terms on one sheet
  • Annual tune-ups produce a short report on the heat exchanger, the burners, the blower and the filter
  • Gas work is signed off by a TSSA-certified technician, which is the record an insurer asks for first

If you own several units in Doon, Lakeshore or near the universities, we keep one file per address so you can pull up any of them in a minute.

Frequently asked questions

Do you work with landlords in Kitchener-Waterloo?

Yes. We service and replace heating and cooling in rental houses, duplexes and basement units, and we can coordinate visits with your tenants once you approve it.

What is the most low-maintenance heating system for a rental property?

A properly sized high-efficiency furnace or cold-climate heat pump with a deep media filter and a yearly tune-up is about as low-maintenance as it gets. Good installation matters more than any single brand.

Do Kitchener Utilities gas customers qualify for heat pump rebates?

Possibly. For gas-heated homes, eligibility depends on the program terms and your utility, and we confirm it before installation. Homes heated by electricity, oil or propane may qualify for up to $7,500.

Who can I call when the heat quits at a rental in Kitchener-Waterloo?

Yes. We take emergency calls evenings, weekends and holidays. Call 1-647-694-5845 and we'll give you an honest estimate of when a technician can arrive.

How do I choose an HVAC contractor in Kitchener-Waterloo for a rental?

Pick one that puts prices in writing, explains the cause of each repair and sends you records you can keep. That protects you with tenants, insurers and future buyers.

Is a heat pump a good idea for a Waterloo family home?

Often, yes. A cold-climate heat pump replaces your central air and covers most of the heating season, and a hybrid setup keeps the gas furnace for the coldest days.

How much does furnace repair cost in Kitchener-Waterloo?

Furnace repair in Kitchener-Waterloo is quoted after the diagnosis, because the price depends on the part: an ignitor, a flame sensor and a control board all cost different amounts. You see the written price before we fix anything. If replacement makes more sense, a new high-efficiency furnace starts from $3,300 installed, and installed pricing depends on size and installation requirements.
