Repair or replace? What an HVAC technician in Richmond Hill looks at first

Age alone doesn't settle it. These are the checks we run before we give you an answer.

  • The real age of the furnace and AC, read off the rating plate
  • Heat exchanger condition, including any cracks or rust
  • Repair history and how many parts have failed in recent years
  • The cost of this repair against the value left in the unit
  • Refrigerant type, since older R-22 systems are costly to recharge
  • Efficiency: an older mid-efficiency furnace against a new 96% two-stage model
  • Whether parts for your model are still easy to get
  • Rebates and financing that change the math on replacing

Furnace installation in Richmond Hill when everything hits 25 at once

Here's a pattern we run into a lot. The house went up around 1998. The furnace, the air conditioner, the water heater and the humidifier all went in that same year. Now they're all old, and each one is waiting for its turn to quit.

You can replace them one emergency at a time. Or you can plan it. Planning lets you pick the season, compare options, get rebate pre-approval in place and spread the cost with financing. A furnace that dies in a January cold snap rarely leaves you that kind of choice.

Homes up in Oak Ridges sit on the moraine, which runs a little colder and snowier than the city to the south, so we factor that into sizing. A planned replacement also opens doors a rushed one can't, like trading the old AC for a cold-climate heat pump that works alongside a new furnace.

  • Furnace, coil and outdoor unit matched to work as one system
  • Fresh heat-loss sizing, since many original units were oversized
  • Permits, gas line checks and proper venting
  • Rebate pre-approval in place before install day

Frequently asked questions

How long does a furnace last in a Richmond Hill home?

Most gas furnaces last about 15 to 20 years with regular maintenance, so many original furnaces in 1990s and 2000s Richmond Hill homes are now at or past that range.

Should I repair or replace a 20-year-old furnace?

Replacing usually makes more sense if the repair costs a big share of a new unit or the heat exchanger is cracked. A technician should check the heat exchanger, repair history and efficiency before you decide.

Should I replace my furnace and AC at the same time?

Usually yes, if both are close in age. A matched furnace, coil and outdoor unit work better together, and you only go through one install.

What does furnace installation in Richmond Hill cost?

High-efficiency furnaces start from $3,300 installed. Installed pricing depends on size and installation requirements, and we give you a guaranteed quote in writing.

Is AC repair in Richmond Hill worth it on an old R-22 system?

Sometimes, but recharging an R-22 system is costly because that refrigerant is no longer made. For many older units, a new AC or heat pump is the better use of the money.

How do I plan an HVAC replacement before my system fails?

Book an assessment in spring or fall, get a guaranteed quote, and line up rebate pre-approval and financing before the busy season. That way the swap happens on your schedule, not the furnace's.
