Rooms and homes a mini split installation fixes

Ductless systems solve the spaces central heating and cooling leaves behind. These are the calls we get most.

  • The addition or bonus room over the garage is always the wrong temperature

    The original ductwork was never sized for it. A single-zone mini-split gives that room its own heating and cooling, on its own thermostat, without re-ducting the house.

  • The finished basement is cold and damp

    A wall-mounted or slim-duct head in the basement heats it in winter and dehumidifies it in summer. No more space heaters, no more musty smell.

  • Your bungalow has baseboard heat and no ducts

    One or two ductless heat pump heads can heat and cool the main living space for a fraction of the cost of electric baseboards. Larger homes move up to a multi-zone ductless system.

  • The condo or apartment has no central air

    Where the building allows an outdoor unit on the balcony or wall, a ductless air conditioner installation is the cleanest fix. Quiet, efficient and far better looking than a window unit.

  • The cottage needs heating in spring and fall and cooling in August

    A Midea Hyper Heat ductless heat pump handles both, runs on a standard 240V circuit and keeps running down to -30°C for winter weekends.

  • The garage workshop or home office is unusable half the year

    A small single-zone head keeps a garage, studio or office comfortable year-round without tying it into the house system.

  • Your window AC is loud, ugly and blocks the view

    A ductless mini split AC replaces it with a whisper-quiet wall unit, better efficiency and real heating in winter. The window goes back to being a window.

  • Sound familiar?

    Describe the problem and we'll tell you what it usually is, what it costs to fix, and whether it's worth fixing.

What is included in every EHI ductless mini split installation

  • Sizing of the indoor head to the room's heat load, sun exposure and ceiling height
  • Indoor unit mounted on a level backing plate, with a sealed 3-inch wall sleeve
  • Outdoor unit on a wall bracket or riser stand, above the Ontario snow line and clear of drifts
  • Insulated copper line set, control wiring and a line set cover so nothing dangles down your siding
  • Condensate drain routed outside or to a pump where gravity will not do the job
  • Dedicated 240V circuit and outdoor disconnect by ESA-licensed electricians, panel capacity checked
  • Vacuum to 500 microns, charge verified by licensed refrigeration techs, and a full heating and cooling test
  • Remote and Wi-Fi set-up, warranty registration, and rebate pre-approval and submission if your unit qualifies

Frequently asked questions

How much does ductless mini split installation cost in Ontario?

Ductless mini split installation in Ontario starts at $5,500 installed with EHI for a single-zone Midea Hyper Heat system, including the bracket, line set, condensate drain and 240V circuit. Multi-zone systems with two to five heads are quoted per home, and every quote is a guaranteed final price.

Does a ductless mini split heat in a Canadian winter?

Yes. A cold-climate ductless mini split heat pump like the Midea Hyper Heat is rated to keep heating down to -30°C and holds strong capacity at -15°C, which covers most Ontario winter nights. In Northern Ontario or for very cold snaps, many homeowners keep baseboards or a furnace as backup.

Do ductless heat pumps qualify for the Ontario rebate?

Cold-climate ductless heat pumps can qualify for the Home Renovation Savings Program if the model is on NRCan's qualified products list and pre-approval is in place before installation. The rebate is based on heat pump size and your heating fuel. We check eligibility and handle the paperwork.

How long does it take to install a mini split?

A single-zone ductless mini split installation usually takes four to six hours. Multi-zone systems with several heads take one to two days depending on how many rooms and how far the line sets run.

Can one mini split cool and heat the whole house?

One single-zone head can handle an open-concept main floor or a small bungalow, but closed-off bedrooms and a basement usually need their own heads. For a whole home without ducts, a multi-zone system with two to five indoor units is the better fit.

Do I need an electrician for a mini split installation?

Yes. A ductless heat pump needs its own dedicated 240V circuit and an outdoor disconnect. EHI includes this work, done by ESA-licensed electricians, in the installed price, and we check that your panel has room for the new breaker.
