Furnace and AC trouble our Courtice HVAC technicians solve

These problems tend to show up together once a house reaches its third decade.

  • The AC and the furnace are the same age and both are slowing down

    Replacing them as a matched pair costs less in labour than two separate jobs, and the coil, furnace blower and outdoor unit are sized to work together. You can also swap the AC for a heat pump at the same time.

  • You can't afford to lose a workday to a surprise breakdown

    A planned replacement happens on a day you pick. We book the assessment around your commute, line up the equipment and rebate pre-approval, and schedule the install for a day that suits you.

  • The ducts are fine but the cooling feels weak

    Twenty-year-old ductwork in Courtice is usually still usable. A ducted cold-climate heat pump connects to it in place of the old AC, so you get fresh cooling and efficient heating without tearing into walls.

  • Winter gas bills keep creeping up

    A hybrid system lets a heat pump handle most of the season while the furnace covers the coldest days. Enbridge gas-heated homes commonly qualify for up to $2,000 toward a qualifying heat pump.

  • The price of a full replacement feels out of reach

    We lay out 0% financing for 3, 6 or 9 months and open loans from 2.99% OAC next to the cash price, with rebates already subtracted, so you can see the monthly number before you decide.

  • Wind off the lake makes the family room drafty

    Leaky duct joints and a weak supply run let cold air win. We seal accessible ducts in the basement, check that the room gets enough supply air, and size the new system for the real heat loss.

  • You'd planned to replace everything in the fall, but the AC may not make it through July

    Then we stage it. The heat pump goes in first, on the same ducts, and replaces the AC right away. The furnace follows in the fall as a matched unit, or stays if it tests sound. One assessment, two visits, and the price for both is locked on the first quote so the second step doesn't creep.

  • Sound familiar?

    Describe the problem and we'll tell you what it usually is, what it costs to fix, and whether it's worth fixing.

Spring or fall: when to book a planned replacement in Courtice

If the equipment still runs, you get to choose the season. Each one has a case.

Spring suits a heat pump or AC replacement. The old unit has survived the winter, the new one gets commissioned in cooling mode before the first heat wave, and install dates are easier to pick before the summer rush. Fall suits a furnace, for the same reason in reverse: the new furnace is tested in heating mode while the weather is still mild, and you head into January with fresh equipment and a registered warranty.

What matters more than the month is the lead time. Rebate pre-approval, financing approval and equipment ordering all take a little while, so the ideal call comes a season before you want the work done. For a commuter household in North Courtice or Courtice Shores, that also means we can hold an evening or Saturday slot instead of fitting you in around an emergency.

Frequently asked questions

When should I replace a 25-year-old furnace in Courtice?

The best time is before it fails, ideally in spring or fall. A 25-year-old furnace is past the typical lifespan, so pricing a replacement now gives you time to compare options and line up rebates.

Will my Courtice home's ductwork work with a heat pump?

Usually it will. Ducts in 1980s to 2000s homes usually handle a ducted cold-climate heat pump, though we test static pressure first and fix any weak spots.

What financing do you offer for HVAC in Courtice?

We offer 0% interest for 3, 6 or 9 months, and open loans from 2.99% OAC for longer terms. Rates are subject to lender approval.

Can a Courtice HVAC technician come by in the evening?

Often, yes. We're open until 8 pm Monday to Saturday and 10 am to 5 pm on Sunday, so after-work and weekend assessments are usually possible.

Is it cheaper to swap the furnace and air conditioner in one visit?

Usually, yes. One install costs less in labour than two separate jobs, and matched equipment runs more efficiently together.

What's a realistic budget for furnace installation in Courtice?

Plan on a starting price of $3,300 installed for a high-efficiency gas furnace, or $5,500 for a cold-climate heat pump. Installed pricing depends on size and installation requirements, and we stand behind the quoted price.
