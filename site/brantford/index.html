What a Brantford HVAC contractor should list on a furnace quote

A fair price is a complete price. These are the items our written quotes spell out, so you can compare us line for line with anyone else.

  • The furnace make, model and AFUE rating, not just the words high efficiency
  • BTU sizing from a heat-loss calculation, with the number written down
  • New PVC venting and a condensate drain, including a pump if one is needed
  • Gas line and shut-off checks by a TSSA-certified gas technician
  • Duct connections, return air and any filter cabinet changes
  • Permits, haul-away of the old unit and cleanup of the furnace room
  • Warranty terms and who registers them
  • Any rebate, financing or price-beat details in writing

Air conditioning in Brantford, added to an old house or a new one

Summers here get hot and sticky, and many older homes near downtown still lean on window units. If the house already has ducts for a forced-air furnace, adding central air is usually simple: an outdoor condenser, a coil on top of the furnace and a refrigerant line between the two.

Here's the catch. Ducts in a century home or a 1950s bungalow were sized for heat, and cooling needs more airflow. We check duct sizes and return air before quoting, so the upstairs bedrooms actually cool down instead of just the basement.

In a new west-end build the question changes. The ducts are fine, so the choice is between a plain AC and a heat pump that cools in summer and heats for much of the winter. A heat pump costs more upfront, but it can cut your gas use and may qualify for a rebate. We price both so you can decide with real figures.

  • Heat-gain sizing, not a guess from square footage
  • Coil matched to your furnace and blower
  • Condenser placed away from bedroom windows and the lot line
  • Rebate eligibility confirmed before installation

Frequently asked questions

What will a high-efficiency furnace cost me in Brantford?

Installed prices for our high-efficiency gas furnaces begin at $3,300. Installed pricing depends on size and installation requirements, and the quote is guaranteed in writing.

Can you add central air to an older Brantford home?

Yes, if it has forced-air ducts. We check that the ducts and return air can handle cooling airflow, and suggest a ductless head for any room they can't reach.

Should I get an AC or a heat pump for my new build in West Brant?

A heat pump is often worth pricing, since it cools like an AC and also heats for much of the year. We quote both so you can compare upfront cost, running cost and any rebate.

Do you offer financing on furnace installation in Brantford?

Yes. We offer 0% interest for 3, 6 or 9 months and open loans from 2.99% OAC, subject to lender approval.

Can a quote from another Brantford HVAC company get me a lower price?
