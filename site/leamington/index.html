Before we install: our Leamington HVAC contractor checklist for rural and lakeside lots

Properties outside town raise a few extra questions. We answer them before we price the job.

  • How the home is heated now (propane, oil, electric or gas), since that sets your rebate tier
  • Electrical service size, and whether the panel can take a 240V heat pump circuit
  • Dust from fields and farm traffic near the spot for the outdoor unit
  • Wind exposure and spray on lakefront lots
  • A raised, level pad that keeps the unit above standing water
  • Distance from bedrooms, and from any workshop or barn equipment
  • Condensate drain routes that won't freeze or pool by the foundation
  • Whether a seasonal place needs low-temperature settings while you're away

Heat pump installation in Leamington when there's no gas at the road

Past the edge of town, plenty of homes heat with propane or oil, and a long cooling season means a separate AC working hard all summer as well. A cold-climate heat pump can replace that AC and take over most of the heating, all from one outdoor unit.

That matters for rebates. Homes heated by propane, oil, wood or electricity may qualify for the higher tier of the Home Renovation Savings Program, up to $7,500 based on heat pump size. Pre-approval has to be in place before installation, and we file it for you.

We size the system from a heat-loss calculation, check that your electrical service can carry it and plan the backup heat. Many owners keep the existing furnace for the odd deep-cold night, while others go all-electric with an air handler.

  • Midea EVOX G3 and Kepler units rated to -30°C
  • Panel and 240V circuit handled through ESA-licensed electrical work
  • Backup heat planned, never left to chance
  • Rebate paperwork handled from start to finish

Summer trouble an HVAC technician in Leamington sees every year

A cooling season that runs from May into October is hard on equipment. These are the calls that come in once the humidity settles over the county.

  • The AC runs for five months straight and quits in the hottest week

    Long seasons wear out capacitors and contactors sooner than they do up north. A spring tune-up catches a weak capacitor before August does, and when a part fails anyway, our licensed refrigeration techs diagnose it and give you a written price before the repair.

  • Greenhouse and field dust pack the outdoor coil solid

    A coil caked with dust can't shed heat, so the unit runs longer and hotter. We rinse the coil at every service visit, set the unit on a raised pad away from laneways where trucks kick up dust, and suggest a filter schedule that suits a farm property.

  • The farmhouse heats with propane and has a window unit in every bedroom

    One cold-climate heat pump on the existing ducts, or a multi-zone ductless system where there are none, replaces the window units and takes over most of the heating. Propane-heated homes may land in the higher rebate tier, and the furnace can stay as backup.

  • The house near the lake is sticky at 22°C

    An oversized air conditioner cools fast and shuts off before it pulls the moisture out. We size from a heat-gain calculation and favour inverter equipment that runs long and low, which dries the air as it cools.

    Describe the problem and we'll tell you what it usually is, what it costs to fix, and whether it's worth fixing.

Frequently asked questions

Will a heat pump cut propane use on a Leamington farm property?

Yes, through most of the year. A cold-climate heat pump sized from a heat-loss calculation can carry most of the heating, and propane-heated homes may qualify for up to $7,500 in rebates.

Do you install air conditioning in Leamington lakeside homes?

Yes. We install central AC, heat pumps and ductless systems in homes along Lake Erie, with outdoor units raised and placed out of the worst wind.

How do I keep my AC going through a long, hot summer down here?

Book a tune-up every spring and keep the outdoor coil clear of dust and debris. A clean coil and a correct refrigerant charge make the biggest difference.

If my AC dies on a Saturday, can I get AC repair in Leamington?

Yes. We take emergency calls evenings, weekends and holidays, and you'll get a written price before the repair starts.

Do you serve Kingsville and Wheatley too?

Yes. We book visits in Leamington, Kingsville, Wheatley and nearby Essex County communities from our Stouffville office.

Will an older farmhouse panel handle a heat pump?

Sometimes. Many homes have room for a 240V circuit, but older or smaller panels may need an upgrade, which we check during the visit.

How much does AC repair or a new AC cost in Leamington?

AC repair is priced after the diagnosis, because the part sets the cost, and you approve the written price before we fix anything. A new central air conditioner gets a written quote after a heat-gain calculation, and a cold-climate heat pump that also heats starts from $5,500 installed.
