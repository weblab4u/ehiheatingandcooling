Air conditioning in Vaughan that can't keep up, and other big-home headaches

Large homes and commercial spaces run into the same handful of issues. Here's what we find and how we fix it.

  • The great room with two-storey windows is the hottest spot in the house

    Tall glass takes on a huge amount of sun, and one floor register can't push enough cool air up that high. A high-wall ductless head or a dedicated zone handles that load directly.

  • The upstairs AC runs nonstop on a heat-wave afternoon

    In two-system homes, the upper unit is often the undersized one. We run a heat-gain calculation, check the attic duct runs and resize the equipment to what the floor actually needs.

  • The far bedrooms are stuffy while the kitchen is cold

    Long duct runs lose pressure before they reach the end of the house. A multi-zone ductless system can serve several rooms from one outdoor unit, each with its own control.

  • The work area by the loading door in Concord never warms up

    Big doors dump heat every time they open. We look at the space, the doors and the equipment already there, then recommend capacity that recovers quickly after each opening.

  • Heating a big house costs more every winter

    An old single-stage furnace wastes a lot of gas in a large home. A two-stage, variable-speed furnace up to 120,000 BTU, or a hybrid heat pump setup, cuts that waste.

  • Outdoor units and line sets clash with the look of a custom home

    We plan line set routes before install day, use paintable covers and set condensers out of sight lines where the lot allows, so the system suits the house.

  • Sound familiar?

    Describe the problem and we'll tell you what it usually is, what it costs to fix, and whether it's worth fixing.

Frequently asked questions

How many HVAC systems does a large Vaughan home need?

It depends on the load calculation, but big homes with tall ceilings and lots of glass often need two systems, or one main system plus multi-zone ductless for the hardest rooms to cool.

What is a multi-zone ductless system?

A multi-zone ductless system is one outdoor heat pump connected to several indoor heads, each with its own temperature control. It suits large homes, additions and rooms the ductwork can't reach.

Do you do commercial HVAC in Vaughan?

Yes. We install and service rooftop units, split systems, ductless and heat pumps for shops, showrooms, offices and warehouses in Concord and across Vaughan, and we can work around your hours.

Can a heat pump cool a big house during a Vaughan heat wave?

Yes, when it's sized from a proper heat-gain calculation. A cold-climate heat pump cools like central air in summer and heats down to -30°C in winter.

What if my furnace or AC quits in Vaughan after hours?

Yes. We take emergency HVAC calls in Vaughan evenings, weekends and holidays. Call 1-647-694-5845 to reach us.

Can one HVAC company in Vaughan look after my house and my shop?

Yes. We handle residential and commercial work, so one company can look after your home and your Concord unit. Each one gets its own assessment and quote.
