Privacy Policy

Last updated October 2026 · Efficient Home Improvements (EHI Heating And Cooling)

Efficient Home Improvements, operating as EHI Heating And Cooling, respects your privacy and complies with Canada's Personal Information Protection and Electronic Documents Act (PIPEDA). This policy explains what we collect and why.

What we collect

When you request a quote, call us or submit a form we collect your name, phone number, email, address or city, and details about your home and HVAC equipment. For rebate applications we collect the information the Home Renovation Savings Program requires, such as heating fuel, utility account details and equipment model and serial numbers. For financing we pass your application to our lending partner with your consent.

How we use it

To respond to your request, prepare quotes, schedule and perform work, register warranties, submit rebate applications on your behalf, and follow up about your service. We do not sell your personal information.

Sharing

We share information only with parties needed to deliver the service: equipment manufacturers (warranty registration), rebate program administrators (Save on Energy, Enbridge Gas), lenders (financing you apply for), and service providers who host our website and forms.

Website data

This site may use cookies and analytics to understand how visitors use it. You can disable cookies in your browser. Form submissions are transmitted over HTTPS.

Your rights

You can ask to see, correct or delete the personal information we hold about you by emailing efficienthomecomfort@gmail.com or calling 1-647-694-5845.
