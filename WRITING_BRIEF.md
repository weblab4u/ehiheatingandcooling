## Voice and style (strict)
- Native Canadian English, Ontario homeowner conversation. "You" and "we". Canadian spelling: colour, labour, neighbour, centre, metre, licence (noun), programme is NOT used (use program). Celsius, "hydro bill", "furnace room", "cottage", "GTA", "Hydro One", "Enbridge".
- Grade 10 readability. Mostly short sentences. Mix in a few longer ones. Vary paragraph length. One idea per paragraph.
- Sales copy, problem-first: name the homeowner's problem, then the fix, then the benefit. Every section earns its place. No fluff, no throat-clearing, no "In today's world".
- Be conversational and warm, lightly humorous where natural (one light line per page max). Use plain idioms Ontarians use ("the dead of a January cold snap", "muggy July nights").
- Show experience: concrete details (BTU sizing, -30°C ratings, Manual J heat-loss, condensate drains, 240V circuit, refrigerant lines, AFUE, HSPF2, SEER2, two-stage, variable-speed).
- E-E-A-T: mention who does the work (TSSA-certified gas techs, licensed refrigeration techs), what we check, what we hand over (permits, warranty registration, rebate paperwork).
- Write for AI citation too: give direct, quotable answers. Start FAQ answers with a complete sentence that answers the question. Include clear definitions ("A cold-climate heat pump is...") and numbers where we have them.

## Hard bans
- NO em dashes (—) or en dashes (–). Use commas, periods, or "to" for ranges ("2 to 5 ton").
- Use fewer than 3 times per page: unique, ensure, utmost.
- Never use: meticulous, meticulously, navigating, navigate (as metaphor), complexities, realm, bespoke, tailored, towards, underpins, ever changing, ever-evolving, the world of, not only, seeking more than just, designed to enhance, it's not merely, our suite, it is advisable, daunting, in the heart of, when it comes to, in the realm of, amongst, unlock the secrets, unveil the secrets, robust, elevate, seamless, seamlessly, leverage, delve, cutting-edge, state-of-the-art, game-changer, look no further, whether you're, rest assured, peace of mind (max once per page), top-notch, hassle-free, we've got you covered (max once), in conclusion, furthermore, moreover, additionally.
- No exclamation marks except at most one per page.
- No "near me" literally in body copy as a phrase unless natural ("looking for a furnace installer near you" is fine).
