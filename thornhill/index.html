  • DuctworkOriginal and often tired

Airflow problems in 40-year-old Thornhill homes, and the fixes

When the furnace gets replaced but the ducts don't, these complaints tend to stick around.

  • Some registers barely push any air

    Old duct joints separate over time, and air leaks into the basement ceiling instead of reaching the room. We find the leaks, seal them with mastic or foil tape, and reconnect or replace crushed runs.

  • The new furnace is louder than the old one ever was

    A modern blower pushing through small or restricted ducts creates high static pressure, and you hear it as whistling or rushing air. We measure it, then add return capacity or open the restriction so the blower can run at a quieter speed.

  • Dust is back on the shelves a day after you wipe them

    Leaky return ducts pull dusty air from the basement and wall cavities. Sealing the returns and fitting a proper media filter cabinet keeps that dust out of the living space.

  • Two floors share one big return grille

    Homes of this age were often built with very few returns, so bedrooms with the door closed starve for air. Adding returns or transfer grilles lets air get back to the furnace and evens out the rooms.

  • The AC coil ices over on hot afternoons

    Low airflow across the coil is a common cause, along with a clogged filter or low refrigerant. Our licensed refrigeration techs check all three before recommending a repair or a new matched system.

  • Repairs on the 1990s furnace keep piling up

    Ignitors, inducer motors and control boards tend to fail in clusters at that age. Our TSSA-certified gas techs check the heat exchanger and repair history, then tell you plainly whether a repair still makes sense.

    Describe the problem and we'll tell you what it usually is, what it costs to fix, and whether it's worth fixing.

Frequently asked questions

Is Thornhill part of Vaughan or Markham?

Both. Thornhill west of Yonge Street is in Vaughan and the east side is in Markham, and we serve the whole community either way.

Should I fix my ducts before replacing my furnace?

Yes, if the ducts are leaky, crushed or short on returns. A new furnace on bad ducts runs louder and still leaves rooms uneven, so we fix both in the same job.

How do I know if my Thornhill home has a duct problem?

Uneven rooms, weak airflow at some registers, whistling vents and extra dust are the usual signs. A static pressure test confirms it.

What will furnace installation in Thornhill cost with duct repairs?

A high-efficiency furnace in Thornhill starts from $3,300 installed. Installed pricing depends on size and installation requirements, plus any duct repairs, and the quote is guaranteed in writing.

Can a Thornhill HVAC contractor work on a heritage home in Old Thornhill?

Yes. In older houses with little room for ducts we often use ductless or multi-zone heat pumps, which need only small wall openings.

Is there emergency HVAC in Thornhill on both sides of Yonge?

Yes. Our technicians answer after-hours emergency calls on the Vaughan and Markham sides alike, including weekends and holidays. Call 1-647-694-5845.
