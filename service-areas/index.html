A note on rebates by location

The Home Renovation Savings Program applies province-wide, but the rebate tier depends on your heating fuel and utility. Enbridge gas customers sit in one tier; homes on electricity, propane, oil or wood (common in cottage country and Northern Ontario) sit in the higher tier, up to $7,500. Some local hydro utilities add their own incentives. We check all of it for your address. See the Ontario heat pump rebate guide.

Northern homeowners: cold-climate heat pumps rated to -30°C, like the Midea EVOX G3 and Kepler, are built for Sudbury, North Bay and Timmins winters. We'll still recommend a backup heat source and size it properly for your design temperature.
