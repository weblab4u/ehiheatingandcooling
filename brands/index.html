Why we're picky about the brands we carry

Plenty of HVAC contractors will install whatever the supplier has on the shelf that week. We carry four brands on purpose, and each one had to clear the same four tests before we put our name beside it.

Parts you can get in Ontario. A heat pump is only as good as the part you need on a Sunday in January. We chose lines where we have dealer access to parts and technical support, so a repair is a day, not a month.

Real cold-climate ratings. Midea EVOX G3 and Kepler are rated to heat at -30°C. That matters in Barrie, Sudbury and North Bay, and honestly it matters in Markham too during a cold snap.

On the NRCan qualified products list. The Home Renovation Savings Program only pays out on heat pumps that make the list. We check the exact model and size before we quote, so your up to $7,500 rebate is real and not a hope. Confirm eligibility before installation, and remember program terms can change.

Warranty support that works. Authorized dealers can register your warranty, access extended coverage of up to 12 years parts and labour on select systems, and get a manufacturer on the phone. A contractor buying grey-market equipment online cannot. See our warranty page for how coverage works.

Midea, Lennox and Kepler HVAC, serviced as well as sold

Being a Midea heat pump dealer and Lennox dealer means we also fix what we sell. If you already own Lennox heating and cooling equipment or a Midea system another contractor installed, our TSSA-certified gas techs and licensed refrigeration techs can service it. Book through HVAC repair and maintenance, or call for same-day emergency service if you have no heat or no cooling. Looking for Lennox installers or Kepler HVAC contractors near you in the GTA, Kitchener-Waterloo, Ottawa or anywhere between? That's us.

Frequently asked questions

Is EHI an authorized Midea, Lennox, Kepler and Rinnai dealer in Ontario?

Yes. EHI Heating & Cooling is an authorized dealer for Midea, Lennox, Kepler and Rinnai, serving homeowners across Ontario from the GTA to Northern Ontario. That gives you factory-backed equipment, dealer warranty registration and access to extended coverage on select systems.

Which brand should I pick for a cold-climate heat pump in Ontario?

For most Ontario homes we recommend the Midea EVOX G3 or a Kepler cold-climate heat pump, both rated to heat at -30°C, with Lennox cold-climate options available for homeowners who want to match an existing Lennox furnace. The right pick depends on your heat-loss calculation, ductwork, heating fuel and rebate tier, so we quote the options side by side.

Do you install Midea air conditioners?

Yes, in the form of a Midea heat pump, which cools your home like a central air conditioner in summer and heats it in winter. A Midea ductless mini-split does the same for a single room or a home without ducts. Visit our Midea heat pump page or AC installation page to compare.

Are Midea, Kepler and Lennox heat pumps eligible for the Ontario heat pump rebate?

A heat pump is eligible for the Home Renovation Savings Program rebate when the specific model and size appear on NRCan's qualified products list, and many Midea, Kepler and Lennox cold-climate models do. We confirm eligibility for your exact model before installation and submit pre-approval first, because the program will not pay for equipment installed before approval. Program terms can change, so check our rebate guide.

What's the difference between an authorized dealer and any contractor who can buy the equipment?

An authorized dealer is trained by the manufacturer, registers your warranty correctly, has direct access to parts and technical support, and can offer extended coverage that an unauthorized installer cannot. Equipment installed by a non-authorized contractor may carry a shorter warranty or none at all, so ask any installer which brands they are authorized for.

Do you repair Lennox and Midea systems you didn't install?

Yes. We repair and maintain Lennox furnaces, Lennox air conditioners, Midea heat pumps and Rinnai tankless water heaters regardless of who installed them. Diagnostics come with a written quote before any work, and same-day service is available across most of our Ontario service area.
