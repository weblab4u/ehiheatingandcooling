Why homeowners ask us for Goodman

Goodman is an American HVAC manufacturer and part of the Daikin group. It has built its name on straightforward, well-made heating and cooling equipment at a fair price, and that's exactly why people ask us for it. If you want a dependable furnace or central air system without paying for features you'll never use, Goodman belongs on your shortlist.

Simple to service. Goodman equipment is common across North America, so parts are easy to find and our techs know it well. That keeps repair visits short.

Strong factory warranties. Goodman's better furnaces carry a heat exchanger limited warranty for as long as you own your home, plus 10-year unit replacement and 10-year parts limited warranties. The catch: you have to register online within 60 days of installation. We do that for you before we leave, so your coverage is on file from day one.

Matched systems. A Goodman furnace, coil and outdoor unit are designed to work together, which is what gets you the rated efficiency. We match the indoor and outdoor pieces from the spec sheet, not from whatever is on the truck.

Not sure Goodman is the right fit? We also carry Lennox furnaces, Midea cold-climate heat pumps and Kepler heat pumps, and we're happy to quote them side by side.

Which Goodman system is right for your home

GMVC96: the quiet, even-heat pick

Two-stage heat runs on low most of the winter, and the variable-speed blower ramps up gently. Best if you want steady temperatures, quiet airflow and a strong partner for central air.

High-efficiency furnaces

GMEC96: two-stage on a tighter budget

The same 96% AFUE and two-stage burner with a multi-speed ECM blower instead of variable-speed. A sensible upgrade from an old 80% furnace without the top-tier price.

Furnace installation

GSXH5: central air that just works

A dependable central air conditioner for homes with ductwork. Pair it with a Goodman furnace and a matched coil and you have a complete heating and cooling system.

AC installation

GSZH5: heating and cooling in one

Cools like central air in summer and heats through the shoulder seasons. Paired with a gas furnace as a hybrid, the furnace takes over on the coldest nights.

Problems a new Goodman system solves

  • Your old 80% furnace is burning through gas

    A Goodman GMVC96 or GMEC96 at up to 96% AFUE puts far more of every gas dollar into your home. On an Ontario winter gas bill, that difference adds up every month.

  • The house swings from too hot to too cold

    Single-stage furnaces blast at full fire, then shut off. Goodman's two-stage gas valve runs on low for long, steady cycles that hold the temperature where you set it.

  • The AC can't keep the upstairs bedrooms cool

    Often it's the wrong size or a mismatched coil. We run a heat-gain calculation and match a Goodman GSXH5 condenser to the right coil so it cools the whole house, not just the basement.

  • You want cooling and some heat from one outdoor unit

    A Goodman GSZH5 heat pump replaces a central air conditioner and adds heating for spring and fall, so the furnace runs less.

  • Your Goodman system is old and the repair quote is big

    Once a furnace or AC is past about 15 years and needs a major part, replacement usually wins on math. We give you both prices in writing so you can decide.

  • Sound familiar?

    Describe the problem and we'll tell you what it usually is, what it costs to fix, and whether it's worth fixing.

What's included in a Goodman installation

  • Heat-loss and heat-gain calculation to pick the right Goodman size
  • Removal and disposal of your old furnace, air conditioner or heat pump
  • PVC intake and exhaust venting and a condensate drain for the condensing furnace
  • Gas connection, leak test and combustion check by TSSA-certified gas technicians
  • Matched indoor coil, new line set where needed, vacuum and refrigerant charge by licensed refrigeration techs
  • New pad or wall bracket for the outdoor unit, plus electrical disconnect by licensed electrical work
  • Thermostat set-up and a walk-through of your new system
  • Goodman warranty registration online within the 60-day window, and permits where required

Goodman furnace and AC repair, maintenance and service

Already own a Goodman furnace or air conditioner? We repair and maintain Goodman equipment of any age, whoever installed it. Our TSSA-certified gas technicians handle no-heat calls, ignitor and flame sensor faults, pressure switch and inducer problems, blower motor failures and control board error codes.

On the cooling side, our licensed refrigeration techs fix Goodman ACs and heat pumps that won't cool, freeze up, leak refrigerant, short cycle or make noise. Every repair starts with a diagnosis and a written quote before any work.

Yearly maintenance keeps a Goodman system efficient and catches small problems before they turn into a January breakdown. Book through our furnace repair and maintenance or AC repair and maintenance pages, or call for emergency HVAC service if you have no heat or no cooling.

Frequently asked questions

Do you install Goodman furnaces in Ontario?

Yes. We install Goodman GMVC96 and GMEC96 two-stage gas furnaces at up to 96% AFUE across Ontario, from the GTA to Northern Ontario. Every install starts with a heat-loss calculation and a written, guaranteed quote.

How much does a Goodman furnace cost installed?

Our gas furnace installs start from $3,300, and the price for a Goodman furnace depends on the size, the model and the installation needs, such as venting, condensate drain and ductwork changes. You get a written, guaranteed quote with the exact model number before any work starts.

What is the difference between the Goodman GMVC96 and GMEC96?

Both are two-stage Goodman gas furnaces at up to 96% AFUE with 40,000 to 120,000 BTU/h inputs. The GMVC96 has a variable-speed ECM blower that ramps airflow smoothly and works well with central air, while the GMEC96 uses a multi-speed ECM blower and usually costs less.

Do I need to register my Goodman warranty?

Yes. Goodman's longer limited warranties, including 10-year parts coverage, require online registration within 60 days of installation. We register it for you as part of the install so you don't miss the window.

Do you repair Goodman furnaces and air conditioners you didn't install?

Yes. We repair and maintain Goodman furnaces, air conditioners and heat pumps no matter who installed them. You get a diagnosis and a written quote before any work, and same-day service is available across most of our Ontario service area.

Is a Goodman heat pump eligible for the Ontario heat pump rebate?

Only if the exact model and size is on NRCan's qualified products list, which the Home Renovation Savings Program requires. We check your Goodman model before you buy. If it isn't on the list and the rebate matters to you, we'll quote a cold-climate model that is, such as a Midea or Kepler heat pump.

Is Goodman a good brand for Ontario homes?

Goodman makes dependable, fairly priced equipment that suits many Ontario homes, especially when you want a solid two-stage furnace and central air without top-tier pricing. What matters most is correct sizing, a matched coil and a clean install, which is where we focus.
